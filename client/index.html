How to Play Flip 7

The Goal

Score the most points by drawing cards. Game ends when a player reaches the target score!

Gameplay

  • Hit: Draw a card from the deck.
  • Stay: Lock in your current score and wait for the round to end.
  • Busting: If you draw a number card that you already have in your hand, you BUST! You lose all points for the round and are out until the next round.

Card Types

  • Number Cards (0-12): Add their value to your score. The number of cards in the deck equals their value (e.g., there are ten 10s, but only one 1).
  • Modifiers: (+2, +4, +6, etc. and x2) Special cards that increase your total score. These do not count towards your unique number cards.
  • Action Cards: Special cards that mess with your opponents or save you.

Special Actions

  • ❄️ Freeze: Force an active opponent to instantly STAY.
  • 🃏 Flip Three: Force an opponent to draw 3 times on their turn.
  • 🔄 Second Chance: Automatically saves you from busting once.

The Flip 7 Bonus

If you manage to collect 7 unique number cards without busting, the round instantly ends and you receive a 15 point bonus on top of your hand score!
